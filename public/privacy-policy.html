1. Who We Are

FlashMed is a technology platform that operates a mobile application and website (flashmed.in) connecting customers with independent licensed third-party service providers, including pharmacies, blood banks, and delivery partners. FlashMed acts as an intermediary as defined under Section 2(w) of the Information Technology Act, 2000.

For all matters related to data protection, FlashMed acts as the Data Fiduciary under the DPDP Act, 2023 in respect of personal data collected directly through the Platform. The Data Fiduciary contact details are provided at the end of this Policy.

2. Definitions

  • "Personal Data" — any information about an individual that can directly or indirectly identify that individual
  • "Sensitive Personal Data" — passwords, financial information, health data, biometric data, sexual orientation, medical records, and any other information classified as sensitive under applicable law
  • "Data Principal" — the individual to whom the personal data relates (you, the user)
  • "Data Fiduciary" — the entity that determines the purpose and means of processing personal data (FlashMed)
  • "Data Processor" — any third party that processes personal data on behalf of FlashMed (e.g., Firebase, Cloudinary, Cashfree)
  • "Processing" — any operation performed on personal data, including collection, storage, use, sharing, and deletion
  • "Consent" — your free, specific, informed, and unambiguous agreement to processing of your personal data

Device & Technical Data Automatic

Automatically collected to ensure platform security and improve performance.

  • Browser type (for website access)

Usage & Behavioral Data Automatic

Used to improve features and detect fraud.

6. Lawful Basis for Processing

Under the DPDP Act, 2023, we process your data only when we have a lawful basis. The applicable bases are:

9. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures consistent with the SPDI Rules, 2011 and DPDP Act, 2023:

  • Encryption in transit: All communication uses TLS 1.2 or higher
  • Encryption at rest: Sensitive data (prescriptions, identity documents) encrypted in storage
  • Access control: Strict role-based access; only authorized personnel can access user data
  • Authentication: OTP-based login; no password storage on our servers
  • Payment security: Card and banking details handled exclusively by PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processors
  • Database security: Firestore security rules, regular security audits
  • Vulnerability management: Regular dependency updates, security patching
  • Audit logs: All data access events logged and monitored
  • Backup and recovery: Encrypted backups with disaster recovery procedures

9.1 Security Limitations

While we implement industry-standard security, no system is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted over the internet. By using FlashMed, you acknowledge this limitation. You can help by:

  • Not sharing your OTP with anyone
  • Logging out of shared devices
  • Reporting suspicious activity to support@flashmed.in immediately
  • Keeping your phone secure with biometric/PIN lock
  • Updating the FlashMed app to the latest version

9.2 Data Breach Notification

In the unlikely event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will:

  • Notify the Data Protection Board of India within 72 hours of becoming aware
  • Notify affected users without undue delay via email and in-app notification
  • Describe the nature of the breach, data affected, and remediation steps
  • Provide support to mitigate any potential harm

10. Your Rights as a Data Principal

Under the DPDP Act, 2023, you have the following rights:

Your Rights — Quick Reference

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of your personal data we hold
  • Right to Correction: Update inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time for processing based on consent
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: File a grievance with our Grievance Officer
  • Right to Nominate: Nominate another individual to exercise your rights in case of incapacity or death
  • Right to Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interest in certain circumstances
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format

10.1 How to Exercise Your Rights

  1. Email support.flashmed247@gmail.com with subject "Data Rights Request"
  2. Specify which right you wish to exercise
  3. Provide your registered phone number for identity verification
  4. We will respond within 30 days (15 days for simple requests like access)
  5. If we cannot fulfill your request (e.g., due to legal retention), we will explain why in writing

10.2 No Discrimination for Exercising Rights

We will not discriminate against you, deny service, charge extra, or reduce service quality because you exercised your privacy rights.

17. Account Deletion

You have the right to delete your FlashMed account at any time.

17.1 How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the FlashMed app
  2. Go to Profile → Danger Zone → "Delete My Account"
  3. Confirm twice (this action is irreversible)
  4. Alternatively, email support.flashmed247@gmail.com with subject "Account Deletion Request"

17.2 What Happens When You Delete Your Account

  • Your profile, preferences, and addresses are deleted within 7 days
  • Your prescriptions are deleted within 30 days, except those linked to active or recently completed orders (retained 2 years for legal compliance)
  • Your order history is anonymized after 30 days but retained in anonymized form for tax/legal compliance
  • Your blood donor registration (if applicable) is deactivated immediately and deleted within 7 days
  • Push tokens, device IDs, and location history are deleted within 7 days
  • Active referral relationships are anonymized so referees are not affected

17.3 Data We Cannot Delete Immediately

By law, we must retain certain records even after account deletion:

  • Payment transaction records — 8 years (RBI/Income Tax Act)
  • Order details with prescriptions — 2 years (Drugs and Cosmetics Act)
  • Tax-related documents — as required by Income Tax Act, 1961
  • Records pending legal proceedings or regulatory investigation

This retained data is encrypted, access-restricted, and used only for the legal purpose stated.
